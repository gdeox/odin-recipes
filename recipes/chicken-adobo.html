Chicken Adobo

Chicken adobo is a classic Filipino dish of chicken simmered in a savory blend of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices. Known as unofficial national dish of the Philippines, it's cherished for its tender meat, rich flavor, and comforting aroma.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken (thigh or drumsticks)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorn
  • 1 cup water
  • 1-2 tbsp cooking oil
  • salt to taste

Steps

  1. In a bowl, marinate the chicken with soy sauce, crushed garlic, and pepper for at least 30 minutes (optional: overnight for more flavor)
  2. Heat oil in a pan and saute onion until translucent.
  3. Add the marinated chicken (reserve marinade) and sear lightly on all sides.
  4. Pour in the vinegar, reserved marinade, water, and bay leaves. Do not stir immediately after adding vinegar-let it simmer for 1-2 minutes.
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 30-40 minutes until chicken is tender.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt or additional soy sauce. Simmer uncovered for another 5-10 minutes to thicken the sauce if desired.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice.

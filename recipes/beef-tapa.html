Beef Tapa

Beef tapa is a Filipino dish made of thin, marinated beef, pan-fried until tender and savory. Originally a traditional method of preserving meat, it evolved into a beloved part of Filipino breakfast, especially in the classic tapsilog trio.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup calamansi juice
  • 1 head garlic, peeled and minced
  • 2 tbps sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1.5 kg beef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • canola oil

Steps

  1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, calamansin juice, garlic, salt, sugar, and ground black pepper. Stir until sugar and salt are dissolved.
  2. Add beef and massage to fully coat with marinade. Keep overnight in the refrigerator to cure.
  3. Drain meat from marinade.
  4. In a wide pan over medium heat, heat about 1 tbsp of oil.
  5. Add the marinated beef into the pan, including a bit of the marinade.
  6. Let it simmer until the liquid reduces.
  7. Once the liquid evaporates, add a little more oil.
  8. Continue cooking until the beef is browned and slightly crispy. or stop early if you prefer it tender.
  9. Serve hot with garlic rice and sunny-side-up (optional).

Pork Sinigang

Pork sinigang is a Filipino soup made with tender pork simmered in a sour tamarind broth with assorted vegetables. Rooted in early Philippine cooking traditions. Sinigant is known for its comforting, tangy flavor and remains one of the country's most icon home-style dishes.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or shoulder, cut into cubes
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 pack tamarind soup base (or 1 cup fresh tamarind juice)
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 cup string beans, trimmed
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong)
  • 2-3 green chili peppers
  • salt and fish sauce to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pot, bring water to boil. Add pork, onions, and tomatoes. Simmer for 45-60 minutes or until pork is tender.
  2. Add the tamarind soup base (or tamarind juice) and stir well.
  3. Add radish and cook for 5 minutes. Then add string beans and eggplant, simmer for another 3-5 minutes.
  4. Season with salt or fish sauce to taste. Add green chili if desired.
  5. Just before serving, stir in water spinach and cook until wilted. Serve hot with steamed rice.
